Special Features

  • To impart educations through English Medium (C.B.S.E.) from nursery to std. X
  • To educate the little children psychologically with the help of audio-visual and ultra modern equipments in smart classes.
  • Teaching through highly qualififed and well experienced teachers (30:1) Students:Teachers
  • To build up children of high character, integrity, thought and personality in the best interest of the country.
  • Well-equipped attractive library having children book on different subjects
  • Modern computer lab with many computers.
  • We encourage sports & games at school.
  • The number of seats being limited, admission will be made on the basis of merit.
  • A grand building with many storeys and a play ground.
  • Fresh drinking water (R.O.) availability for children.
  • Separate washrooms and toilets for boys and girls students.
  • Separate classes facilities from N.C. to X
  • Some informal examinations like Hand Art competition and cultural programmes are held for throughout development of children at a regular interval.
