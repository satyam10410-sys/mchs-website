TEACHER RECRUITMENT IS OPEN FOR ALL SUBJECTS AND ALL CLASSES

STEP BY STEP GUIDE FOR BECOMING A TEACHER

1

SCHOOL VISIT

Visit our school at the given location and go into the office and show your CURRICULUM VITAE.

2

THE JOB APPLICATION

The concerned ones are given a blank page to write a job application

3

DEMO CLASS

You are requested to give a demo class for 3 days whose payment will be done if you are selected.

APPROVAL BY STUDENT

AGREEMENT & SALARY

6

TAKE CLASS
