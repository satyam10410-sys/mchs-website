MERIDIAN CONVENT HIGH SCHOOL

Every Child is the wealth of the nation.

Meridian Convent High School, located in Kanti Factory Road, founded on 16th January, 2005 aims to impart education through English Medium (C.B.S.E.) from std. Nursery to std. X. It also wants to educate the little children psychologically with the help of audio-visual and ultra modern equipments in smart classes. Teachers are highly qualified and well experienced. The institution targets to build up children of high character, integrity, thought and personality in the best interest of the country. Well-equipped attractive library have books for children on different subjects. Modern computer lab aids in the adding of interest towards the growing AI world. It encourages sports and games at school. The classes are well lit, airy and big.
